Photo Finder Pro 1.0.1
======================

ENGLISH
-------
Finds all photos of one person in your photo folders (face matching) and copies the matches to a folder you choose.

Two ways to get it (you only need one):
- Setup (recommended): double-click PhotoFinderPro-1.0.1-x64-Setup.exe and follow the wizard. No admin rights needed. It adds a Start menu entry; uninstall from Windows Settings -> Apps.
- Portable: double-click PhotoFinderPro-1.0.1-x64-Portable.exe. Nothing is installed. The first start takes a few seconds while it unpacks.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" (SmartScreen), because this app is not code-signed.
Click "More info" -> "Run anyway". This only appears the first time.

- Works fully offline. Nothing is uploaded; there is no tracking and no update check.
- It only READS your photos and COPIES the matches to the output folder. It never deletes, moves or changes your originals.
- Its data (database, thumbnails, settings) is stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\PhotoFinderPro . Uninstalling keeps it (and your photos). To remove everything, also delete that folder.
- Third-party licenses: see THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt.

中文(简体)
-----------
通过人脸比对,在您的照片文件夹中找出同一个人的所有照片,并复制到您指定的文件夹。

有两种使用方式(任选其一):
- 安装版(推荐):双击 PhotoFinderPro-1.0.1-x64-Setup.exe,按向导操作。无需管理员权限,会创建开始菜单快捷方式;如需卸载,请在 Windows“设置 → 应用”中进行,或使用开始菜单里的卸载快捷方式。
- 便携版:双击 PhotoFinderPro-1.0.1-x64-Portable.exe,不会安装任何内容。首次启动需要几秒钟解压。

Windows 可能会显示“Windows 已保护你的电脑”(SmartScreen),因为本程序没有代码签名。
请点击“更多信息”->“仍要运行”。这只会在首次运行时出现。

- 完全离线运行:不上传任何内容,没有追踪,也没有自动检查更新。
- 程序只会“读取”您的照片,并把匹配到的照片“复制”到输出文件夹,绝不会删除、移动或修改您的原始照片。
- 程序数据(数据库、缩略图、设置)保存在 %LOCALAPPDATA%\PhotoFinderPro。卸载时会询问是否一并删除,默认保留;您的照片始终不受影响。
- 第三方许可:请查看 THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt。
