Photo Finder Pro 1.0.0
======================

ENGLISH
-------
Finds all photos of one person in your photo folders (face matching) and copies the matches to a folder you choose.

1. Unzip the whole zip file to any folder (e.g. Desktop). Do not run it from inside the zip.
2. Open the folder and double-click PhotoFinderPro.exe.
3. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" (SmartScreen), because this app is not code-signed.
   Click "More info" -> "Run anyway". This only appears the first time.

- Works fully offline. Nothing is uploaded; there is no tracking and no update check.
- It only READS your photos and COPIES the matches to the output folder. It never deletes, moves or changes your originals.
- Its data (database, thumbnails, settings) is stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\PhotoFinderPro . To remove everything, delete the app folder and that folder.

中文(繁體 / 廣東話)
-------------------
用人面比對,喺你嘅相簿資料夾搵出同一個人嘅所有相,再複製去你揀嘅資料夾。

1. 將成個 zip 檔案解壓縮到任何資料夾(例如桌面)。唔好喺 zip 入面直接開。
2. 打開資料夾,㩒兩下 PhotoFinderPro.exe。
3. Windows 可能會顯示「Windows 已保護您的電腦」(SmartScreen),因為呢個程式冇數碼簽署。
   請㩒「其他資訊」->「仍要執行」。只會喺第一次出現。

- 完全離線運作:唔會上載任何嘢,冇追蹤,亦冇自動更新檢查。
- 程式只會「讀取」你嘅相,同「複製」配對到嘅相去輸出資料夾;絕對唔會刪除、搬移或修改你嘅原本相片。
- 程式資料(資料庫、縮圖、設定)放喺 %LOCALAPPDATA%\PhotoFinderPro 。想完全移除,刪除程式資料夾同呢個資料夾即可。
- 第三方授權:請睇 THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt。
