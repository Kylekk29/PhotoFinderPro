## English (en-US)

**Product name:** Photo Finder Pro

**Short description** (94 chars):
Find every photo of one person in your folders. Offline, private, and your originals stay safe.

**Description:**

```
Find every photo of one person in your photo folders, without uploading anything.

Give Photo Finder Pro a few reference photos of someone, for example Grandpa at different ages, and point it at your photo folders. It goes through every photo, including sub-folders, and shows you the pictures where that person appears. Matching photos are copied to a results folder so you can find them, share them or print them.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Face detection and matching run entirely on your own PC. The app makes no network connections, has no account, no sign-in, no ads and no telemetry. Your photos and the face data never leave your computer.

YOUR ORIGINALS ARE SAFE
Original photos are only read. They are never changed, moved, renamed or deleted. Matches are copied, every copy is verified, and existing files are never overwritten. Each search gets its own results folder.

SEE RESULTS WHILE IT WORKS
Matches appear live while the scan runs, with progress, speed and time left. You can stop at any time and resume later, and photos that were already scanned are remembered, so a second search is much faster.

REVIEW IN SECONDS
Open a result and press Y for "it's them" or N for "not them". It jumps to the next photo. Marks are only labels: no photo is ever deleted. When you are happy, one click exports the reviewed set.

GETS BETTER WITH USE
Add good results back as reference photos to improve later searches. Photos with several people let you pick the right face.

BUILT FOR REAL PHOTO LIBRARIES
- Works with JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP and HEIC (iPhone) photos
- Drag and drop folders and photos onto the window
- Zoomable, face-centred thumbnails
- Loose, Balanced and Strict presets with adjustable thresholds
- Dark and light themes
- English and Traditional Chinese

GOOD TO KNOW
Face matching is not perfect. It can miss photos or suggest wrong ones, especially with small faces, strong side views, sunglasses or large age gaps, so the review step is there for you to check. Please only search photo collections you own or are allowed to use.

Free and open source (MIT). Source code and support: github.com/Kylekk29/PhotoFinderPro
```

**Features** (each at most 200 chars):
1. Finds the photos of one person in your folders from a few reference photos, sub-folders included.
2. Fully offline: no network connections, no account, no ads, no telemetry. Faces and photos stay on your PC.
3. Original photos are only read. Matches are copied to a results folder; nothing is moved or deleted.
4. Press Y or N to confirm or exclude each result quickly, then export the reviewed set in one click.
5. Live results with progress and time left. Stop and resume any time, and already scanned photos are remembered.
6. Supports JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP and iPhone HEIC photos, with drag and drop.
7. Dark and light themes, English and Traditional Chinese, adjustable match thresholds.

**Search keywords (7, each at most 40 chars):** find photos of a person, face search, photo organizer, offline photo search, family photos, HEIC photos, photo finder

**What's new in this version:**

```
First release on Microsoft Store. Find photos of one person in your folders, fully offline, with fast Y/N review and one-click export.
```
