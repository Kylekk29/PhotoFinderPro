# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

*Photo Finder Pro, last updated 2026-10-18 / 最后更新 2026-10-18*

## English

Photo Finder Pro runs entirely on your own PC.

- **No network.** The app makes no network connections. It has no update check, no analytics and no telemetry, and it does not send anything to the developer or to anyone else.
- **No accounts, no personal data collected.** You do not sign in. The developer collects, receives and shares no personal data.
- **What stays on your device.** To do its job the app stores these files in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PhotoFinderPro`: your settings, a scan index (file paths and numeric face descriptors, which are lists of numbers computed from faces), thumbnails and the reference-face snapshots of your searches. None of this leaves your PC.
- **Your photos.** Original photos are only read, never modified, moved or deleted. When you run a search, matching photos are copied to the output folder you choose, on your own disk.
- **Delete everything.** Uninstall the app and delete the `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PhotoFinderPro` folder (if you installed from the Microsoft Store, the app's data is removed with the app). Copies of photos you exported stay in the folder you chose until you delete them.

Face descriptors are biometric-style data, so please only search photo collections you own or are allowed to use.

**Contact:** open an issue at <https://github.com/Kylekk29/PhotoFinderPro/issues>. If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address.

## 简体中文

Photo Finder Pro 完全在您自己的电脑上运行。

- **没有网络连接。** 本程序不会联网,没有更新检查、分析工具和遥测,也不会向开发者或任何人发送任何数据。
- **没有账户,不收集个人信息。** 无需登录。开发者不会收集、接收或分享任何个人信息。
- **留在您电脑上的数据。** 为了完成搜索,程序会在 `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PhotoFinderPro` 中保存:设置、扫描索引(文件路径和数字化的人脸特征,即由人脸计算出的一串数字)、缩略图,以及每次搜索的参考人脸快照。这些数据全部不会离开您的电脑。
- **您的照片。** 原始照片只会被读取,不会被修改、移动或删除。搜索时,符合条件的照片会被复制到您自己选择的输出文件夹(位于您自己的磁盘上)。
- **删除全部数据。** 卸载程序,再删除 `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PhotoFinderPro` 文件夹(如果是通过 Microsoft Store 安装的,程序数据会随程序一并移除)。您导出的照片副本会保留在您选择的文件夹中,需要自行删除。

人脸特征属于生物特征类数据,请只搜索您拥有或获准使用的照片。

**联系方式:** 请在 <https://github.com/Kylekk29/PhotoFinderPro/issues> 提出问题。如果本政策有变更,新版本会发布在同一位置。
